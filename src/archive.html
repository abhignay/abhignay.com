Development Logs

Monthly/Bi-Monthly Dev Logs, August 2021 - November 2025. Newer updates are in the hopper build log.

LogPeriodWhat happened

25June - July 2025Troubleshot a TFAC r3 board with a dead GNSS radio, then tested the motors on the load cell.
24April - May 2025Start of Lander v3: CAD finished, motors tested, hardware built.

15June - July 2023Third attempt at Air MK1, a blown-up TFAC, and TFAC r2.
14April 2023 - May 2023Watch faces, five case revisions, and the name WatchPi.
13January 2023 - March 2023Started a custom smartwatch project.
12November 2022 - December 2022RunCam mount, a redesigned avionics stack, and a simpler ground control UI.

10September 2022Avionics stack assembled and the first ground control software UI.
9June - August 2022Telemetry radio and GNSS added, a barometer bug fixed, and TFAC r2 designed.
8March - May 2022Data logging finished, Air MK1 CAD, and a launch pad design.

1August 2021Designed and manufactured AltusAer's fins, finished SAFAC layout, CubeSat POC.
