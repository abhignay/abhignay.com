January 2023 - March 2023

Dev Log 13

Not much was done over this period of time. I began working on a new project - my own custom built smartwatch. I decided to base this off of a Waveshare RP2040 + 1.28" LCD display module. My plan was to write software that displays the time and date and other "watch stuff", design a case for the watch and shove a rechargeable battery in it.
