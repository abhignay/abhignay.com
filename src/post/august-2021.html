Dev Log 1

August’s progress has been minimal because there is more homework I need to focus on. The fins for “AltusAer” have been designed and manufactured in 3mm plywood (Fig 1). A CubeSat POC (Proof-Of-Concept) is in the works, this CubeSat's intent is to be a technology demonstrator for my projects as well as a medium to help me understand advanced concepts.
